# Design direction

## World

A calm editorial product page for a personal digital practice: warm cream surfaces, deep brown hero and footer bands, system typography, a burnt-orange signal, and a single signature product object. The page should feel deliberate and calm, with every element earning its place.

## First viewport

The hero opens on the promise “Clarity, built in.” A single luminous interface object carries the identity beside the headline. The visitor sees what the practice is about and one clear action without a dense navigation or decorative pathway.

## Type

- System stack: `-apple-system`, BlinkMacSystemFont, SF Pro fallbacks, Helvetica Neue, Arial.
- Display: large semibold sans-serif with tight optical tracking.
- Body: 17–21px for comfortable reading, with sentence-case labels and controls.

## Palette

- Hero brown `#21130f`
- Hero secondary `#321d15`
- Surface cream `#f5eee6`
- Soft cream `#fbf7f2`
- Primary text `#241914`
- Secondary text `#6d5e55`
- Rule `#d9c9bd`
- Accent orange `#b8482a`

## Structure

Focused one-page flow: floating navigation, hero, capabilities, engagement shapes, three-step process, useful outcomes, fit guidance, notes, FAQ, contact form, oversized closing wordmark, and footer. Keep the content in restrained editorial rows instead of card grids.

## Motion and interaction

Use a short opacity/translate reveal on initial load and a single scroll-triggered rise for the closing wordmark. Keep the process static and scannable. The contact form remains future-backend ready with inline success and error states. Respect reduced-motion preferences.
